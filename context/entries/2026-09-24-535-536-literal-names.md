# 2026-09-24: the client's legal name no longer leaks past the redactor (#535, #536)

Branch `fix/535-536-literal-name-redaction`, base `3286109`. Decision: D-088.

## What was wrong (both tier-1, live, client-to-provider)

- **#535:** `re.escape` turned a stored name's space into a literal U+0020. A
  no-break space, a narrow no-break space or two spaces between the words
  leaked the name.
- **#536:** `\b` anchors made any name ending in "." ("Acme Holdings, Inc.")
  permanently unredactable.

In both cases the payload reached the provider with `counts == {}`.

## What changed

- **`redact.py::_literal_pattern`** is the one place data becomes a pattern. It
  rejoins tokens with `_HSPACE+` and anchors conditionally.
  `redact_org_name` and `_redact_names` use it.
- **`check_separator_classes.py`** gains a second signature: `re.escape`
  outside `_literal_pattern`, resolved by the AST. Each signature prints its
  own cause. There are two new fixture cases.
- **The wrapped-line case** ("Atlas\nDefense") stays a residual by the owner's
  spec. It is pinned by a test marked `xfail(strict=True)`, and D-088 records
  it as the owner's decision.

## Proof

- **Tests first, red on `main`:**
  - separator rows derived from `\s` minus `str.splitlines()`;
  - edge rows;
  - a partial-miss row;
  - an `LLMClient.invoke` row asserting both what the provider received and the
    ledger's `redacted_counts`.
- **Red-on-revert (`scripts/red-on-revert.sh --expect`):**
  - each fix, one at a time, reddens its own rows;
  - removing the gate signature reddens its detection tests;
  - on `main`'s `redact.py` the extended gate fires on exactly the two real
    sites.

## Containment while it lands

The owner keeps the provider key out of the panel and stays in fixture mode,
so nothing egresses.
