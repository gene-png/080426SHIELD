# 2026-10-07 — #839: the DoD Zero Trust catalog is the 2025 DoD CIO roadmap

_#838 PR 3, branch `track4/zt-dod-catalog`. Lands with this PR; the decision
record is **D-109**, and D-107 covers the timing._

**What changed for a reader of `main`.**

- The DoD catalog follows the DoD CIO's 2025 roadmap (25-T-1465): 45
  capabilities with their activities, generated into
  `app/zt/_dod_catalog_data.py` from the committed extraction
  `reference-docs/dod/dod_zt_2025_rows.json`. `extract_zt_sources.py dod`
  re-derives that extraction, and `test_zt_dod_catalog_source.py` holds the
  catalog to it.
- Migration **0064** (data only, after 0063) swaps DAT.06 and DAT.07, inserts
  `DOD.USR.09`, re-keys Risk `source_id` and `linked_controls`, and keeps the
  answers on retired rows, unscored and disclosed with the source "the DoD Zero
  Trust Execution Roadmap".
- **Target caps.** A capability with no Advanced activity has its effective
  target capped at 2. The gap engine applies the cap, and so does Risk
  synthesis, per service and inside #891's per-source loop. A capability with
  no Target activity is disclosed only. Both are disclosed per capability on
  the workspace, the client ZT dashboard and all three deliverable files.
- DoD stage 1 is **"Below Target"**, replacing "Not Started", on every surface.
- Every DoD deliverable carries the approved source note (#736, comment
  6040458893), and directly after it ruling 4's line, "Activity descriptions
  are reproduced as published." (6047873969).
- `test_zt_dod_catalog_source.py` pins the PDF by sha256 and holds the served
  catalog to the 45 capability names typed from it.
  `apps/api/scripts/count_dod_levels.py` re-derives D-109's figures.

- **The store is held to the cap** (review F1, #736 comment 6048561596): both
  PATCH routes refuse a maturity stage above the capability's own maximum with
  a typed 422 (`stage_above_capability_max`), and Run-AI drops one. A stage
  stored before the guard is left as is, counted at the maximum by the score,
  and found by `stages_above_capability_max`.
  `apps/api/scripts/count_zt_stages_above_cap.py` measures them, read-only.
- The client ZT dashboard's response carries `retired_answers` and its approved
  note (#914), and the Risk generate audit row names the capabilities whose
  target the cap lowered (#915). Rendering both waits on the advisor's copy.

**What a released DoD view does now.** Under D-107 it recomputes against the
new catalog: rows retire, DAT.06 and DAT.07 swap, and a gap can close by the cap
alone. The stored files do not change.
